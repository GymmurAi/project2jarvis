# Coaching Session Workflow

## Purpose
Standardised procedure for conducting one-to-one and group PPD coaching sessions with students.

## Session Types
- **One-to-One Coaching**: Individual student focus (30-45 minutes)
- **Group PPD Session**: Tutorial group delivery (45-60 minutes)
- **Target Review**: Focused progress check (15-20 minutes)
- **Intervention Session**: At-risk student support (45-60 minutes)

---

## Pre-Session Preparation (15 mins before)

### 1. Review Student Records
- [ ] Check attendance record (last 4 weeks)
- [ ] Review previous session notes
- [ ] Check subject tutor feedback
- [ ] Review Personal Development Assessment results
- [ ] Note any safeguarding flags

### 2. Prepare Session Plan
- [ ] Select coaching model (GROW/CIGAR/OSKAR)
- [ ] Identify session focus areas
- [ ] Prepare discussion prompts
- [ ] Set session objectives (max 3)
- [ ] Gather relevant resources/materials

### 3. Environment Setup
- [ ] Private, comfortable space for one-to-one
- [ ] Appropriate room for group sessions
- [ ] Resources ready (handouts, activities)
- [ ] Technology checked (for digital resources)

---

## Session Structure

### Opening (5 minutes)
1. **Welcome & Rapport Building**
   - Greet student(s) warmly
   - Check-in on wellbeing
   - Review previous session actions

2. **Set Session Agenda**
   - State purpose clearly
   - Agree objectives with student
   - Set positive tone

### Main Activity (25-40 minutes)

#### For One-to-One Sessions:
1. **Review Progress**
   - Attendance check: "How has attendance been since we last met?"
   - Target review: "Let's look at the targets we set..."
   - Celebrate successes: "What are you most proud of?"

2. **Coaching Conversation** (use selected model)
   
   **GROW Model:**
   - **Goal**: "What do you want to achieve by our next session?"
   - **Reality**: "What is happening right now with your attendance/performance?"
   - **Options**: "What could you do differently? What support do you need?"
   - **Will/Way forward**: "What will you commit to doing? When will you do it?"

   **CIGAR Model:**
   - **Current**: "Describe your current situation with [focus area]"
   - **Ideal**: "What would ideal look like?"
   - **Gaps**: "What's stopping you from reaching ideal?"
   - **Actions**: "What specific steps will you take?"
   - **Review**: "How will we know you've succeeded?"

3. **Target Setting**
   - Use SMART framework (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
   - Student agrees to each target
   - Document in college system
   - Set review date

#### For Group PPD Sessions:
1. **Theme Delivery**
   - Fundamental British Values
   - Prevent Duty
   - Sustainability
   - Equality & Diversity
   - Healthy Relationships & Consent
   - Study Skills
   - Career Preparation

2. **Facilitated Discussion**
   - Pose open questions
   - Encourage peer sharing
   - Manage group dynamics
   - Ensure all voices heard

3. **Activity/Workshop**
   - Case studies
   - Role play
   - Group problem-solving
   - Reflection exercises

### Closing (5-10 minutes)
1. **Summarise Key Points**
   - Recap what was discussed
   - Confirm actions agreed
   - Celebrate progress made

2. **Set Next Steps**
   - Confirm target review date
   - Schedule follow-up if needed
   - Share resources/referrals

3. **Check Understanding**
   - "What will you do differently?"
   - "How confident do you feel about this?"
   - "What support do you need?"

---

## Post-Session Actions (Within 24 hours)

### 1. Update Records
- [ ] Session notes in college system
- [ ] Targets set/updated with dates
- [ ] Attendance recorded
- [ ] Safeguarding concerns flagged (if any)
- [ ] Referrals made (if needed)

### 2. Communications
- [ ] Email summary to student (if appropriate)
- [ ] Notify subject tutors of agreed actions
- [ ] Update Head of School on at-risk students
- [ ] Send parent communication (if required)

### 3. Follow-up Scheduling
- [ ] Set reminder for target review
- [ ] Schedule intervention if needed
- [ ] Book next regular session
- [ ] Add to Learner At Risk meeting agenda (if applicable)

## Quality Standards
- Every student feels heard and valued
- Sessions are student-centered and responsive
- Clear actions with accountability
- Progress is visible and celebrated
- Safeguarding always prioritised
- Records accurate and up-to-date

---

## Escalation Triggers
Immediately escalate to:
- **Designated Safeguarding Lead**: Any safeguarding concern
- **Head of School**: Persistent non-attendance, disciplinary issues
- **Additional Learning Support**: Learning difficulties identified
- **Counselling/Welfare**: Mental health, financial, personal crisis

---

## Session Metrics Tracked
- Attendance rate (%)
- Target completion rate (%)
- Session satisfaction (student self-rating 1-10)
- Progress towards destination (UCAS/career)
- Safeguarding incidents (number)
- Referrals made (number)
